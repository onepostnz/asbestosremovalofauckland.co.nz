Asbestos Roof Removal

Asbestos Roof Removal Auckland

We remove asbestos roofing across Auckland quickly, safely and to strict compliance standards. Licensed crews, full containment and certified disposal.

Get A Free QuoteCall 09 873 7082

Our licensed crews use wet methods, containment, HEPA filtration and certified disposal so your home and neighbours stay protected. We carry current certifications and full insurance, and provide air monitoring, clear documentation and an itemised quote after an on-site assessment.

Because asbestos fibres become dangerous when disturbed, roofing removal can’t be treated like any other home project. Asbestos roof removal requires controlled handling, proper containment and compliant disposal to prevent contamination — DIY attempts risk airborne fibres, legal issues and health consequences for households and neighbours.

We secure and isolate the work area, then clean and certify the site when finished. Timely replacement also improves property safety and value.

Safety note: Fragile or weathered asbestos roofing sheets can release fibres when walked on. Don’t pressure-wash or scrape an asbestos roof — call us first.

  • Sealed work zones, warning signage and controlled waste packaging

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my roof contains asbestos?

Many Auckland roofs installed before the 1990s used asbestos-cement sheeting. The only reliable way to confirm is to have a sample tested, which we can arrange as part of your assessment.

Can you replace the roof after removal?

We focus on safe removal and disposal, and can work alongside your roofer or builder so the new roof goes on without delay.

Is it legal to remove asbestos myself?

Small amounts of non-friable asbestos can be handled by homeowners under strict conditions, but it is dangerous and the rules are complex. Most Auckland homeowners and builders choose a licensed removal team to protect their family, neighbours and legal position.
