Textured Ceiling Asbestos

Textured Ceiling Asbestos Removal Auckland

Stipple and textured ceilings in older homes may contain asbestos. We test and remove them safely before you scrape, paint or renovate.

Get A Free QuoteCall 09 873 7082

Textured ceiling coatings — often called stipple or popcorn ceilings — were widely applied in New Zealand homes and some contained asbestos. Scraping or sanding these ceilings can release fibres throughout the house.

We sample the coating to confirm whether it contains asbestos. If it does, we seal off the room, protect surfaces, remove the coating with wet methods and clean the area with HEPA-filtered equipment.

Once cleared, your ceiling is ready to be re-lined or skimmed safely.

Safety note: Don’t scrape a stipple ceiling to ‘see what’s underneath’ — test it first.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my ceiling has asbestos?

Textured ceilings in homes built before the late 1980s are worth testing. A small sample sent for analysis gives a definite answer.

Is it legal to remove asbestos myself?

Small amounts of non-friable asbestos can be handled by homeowners under strict conditions, but it is dangerous and the rules are complex. Most Auckland homeowners and builders choose a licensed removal team to protect their family, neighbours and legal position.
