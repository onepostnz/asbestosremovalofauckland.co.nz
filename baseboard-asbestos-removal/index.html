Baseboard Asbestos Removal

Baseboard Asbestos Removal Auckland

Old baseboards, wall backing boards and flooring underlays can contain asbestos. Get connected with local contractors to remove them safely.

Get A Free QuoteCall 09 873 7082

Asbestos-containing boards were commonly used for baseboards, wall linings, bathroom backing and flooring underlays in older homes. They are usually discovered mid-renovation, when a wall is opened or a floor is lifted.

If you’ve hit something that looks dodgy, stop work and submit an enquiry. A local, independent contractor can test the material, isolate the area and remove it using wet methods, containment and approved disposal.

The area is left clean and documented so tradespeople can safely continue.

Safety note: Hit something unexpected during a reno? Stop, leave it undisturbed, and get advice before anyone keeps cutting.

What the Contractor Typically Does

  • Material identification and testing
  • Work stopped safely and area isolated
  • Containment suited to the job
  • Wet removal of boards and backing
  • Approved waste disposal
  • Clearance clean for the next trade

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can the work fit around my renovation schedule?

Contractors can usually coordinate with your builder so removal fits into your programme with minimum delay.

Is it legal to remove asbestos myself?

Small amounts of non-friable asbestos can be handled by homeowners under strict conditions, but it is dangerous and the rules are complex. Most Auckland homeowners and builders choose an experienced asbestos contractor to protect their family, neighbours and legal position.

Who actually carries out the work?

This is a referral website. When you submit an enquiry it is passed to a local, independent asbestos contractor, who will contact you, assess the job and provide a quote.
