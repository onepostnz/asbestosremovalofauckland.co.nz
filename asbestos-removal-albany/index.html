Albany asbestos removal FAQs

Do you provide asbestos removal in Albany, Auckland?

Yes. Enquiries from Albany and surrounding North Shore suburbs are passed to local, independent asbestos contractors, including Rosedale, Pinehill, Oteha, Greenhithe, Unsworth Heights, Schnapper Rock, Northcross, Browns Bay and Glenfield.

How much does asbestos removal cost in Albany?

Cost depends on the type of material (roofing, cladding, soffits, ceilings), the quantity, access and disposal. The contractor provides an itemised quote after assessing your Albany property.

Do I need asbestos testing before renovating my Albany home?

If your home was built before about 2000, we strongly recommend testing suspect materials before any renovation, repair or demolition. Testing is the only way to know for certain.

Can you remove an asbestos roof in Albany?

Yes. Local contractors remove asbestos-cement roofing using wet methods, containment and approved disposal, and can coordinate with your roofer for the replacement.
